Judge: "How do you know the bacteria stay inside the loop?"
    "The membrane is a 0.2 micrometre hollow fiber, and Bacillus subtilis cells are larger than that, so they cannot cross. We tested it by plating both sides after a run: the lumen plate grew and the shell plate had no colonies. That is two single platings, with no replicate and no detection limit, so I would call it consistent with retention rather than a log reduction. A seven log retention test is the measurement we would run next."

    評審：「你們怎麼知道細菌真的留在迴路裡？」
    「薄膜是 0.2 µm 的中空纖維，Bacillus subtilis 的菌體比它大，過不去。我們在一次運轉後把兩側都塗盤：管內側長菌，殼側沒有菌落。那是兩次單一塗盤，沒有重複、也沒有偵測極限，所以我會說它『與截留一致』，而不是 log reduction。下一步要做的量測是七個對數的截留測試。」
